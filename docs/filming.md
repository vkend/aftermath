# Saturday Oct 31: filming checklist

Print this. Photos and recordings stay on the laptop under `data/`, which is never committed.

## 1. Safety first

- **The generator stays OFF for every shot near the house**, including the "7 ft from the window" scene. The video
  says so: the near-house shots were filmed with it off.
- If it runs at all: outdoors in the open, **more than 25 ft from every door, window and vent**, exhaust pointing
  away. Never in the garage, shed or porch, even with the door open.
- **Never refuel it hot:** turn it off and let it cool first.
- Keep a battery CO alarm in the house while it runs, and stay out of its exhaust.
- Keep house numbers, street signs, car plates and other people's faces out of shots meant for the video.

## 2. Before you start

- On the Mac: `uv run aftermath serve --keep data/filming`. Every recording and photo sent from the phone is saved
  there, with what the app heard and said in `data/filming/log.jsonl`.
- On the iPhone: open the page (it starts in Hindi), tap **शुरू करें**, and send one door photo with **दरवाज़े की फ़ोटो**.
  The button offers Take Photo or Photo Library. Take Photo leaves the lens out of the photo (seen Oct 29): the coach
  measures it as if at 1× and asks «क्या फ़ोटो लेते समय कैमरा 1× पर था…?». A Camera-app photo picked from Photo Library
  keeps the lens, and also the photo's location, which stays on the laptop with the rest.
- Camera app: photo mode, **1×**, phone upright. Keep the originals: texts and chat apps can strip the lens data.
- Bring a tape measure (30 ft or longer), chalk or a marker for the generator's spot, a pencil and this sheet.

## 3. Door photos

Mark the generator's spot. Stand on it with the phone upright at chest height, level, the door in the middle of the
frame and all of it in view. Tape the distance **from the ground under the phone straight to the door's face**
(square to the door) and write it to the inch. Once per door, tape the **leaf**: the door itself, bottom edge to top
edge, not the frame.

## 4. Rating plate

- **P1–P2:** straight on, close enough to read every number, no glare: one through the app (**जनरेटर के लेबल की
  फ़ोटो**), one with the Camera app.
- **P3:** at an angle of about 45°. **P4:** with glare (sun or flash). **P5:** from farther away.
- Write down what the plate really says (section 7).

## 5. Hindi voice turns, through the app

Real voices set how sure the app must be of what it heard. Tap **बोलें**, say the line, tap **हो गया, भेजें**. Go down
the list in order, each line two or three times, at an everyday volume with the phone at arm's length; some outdoors
with background noise. Ask anyone else before recording them; their recordings stay on the laptop too.

- **Short answers:** हाँ। · नहीं। · हाँ, है। · नहीं है। · हाँ, हैं। · नहीं हैं। · पता नहीं। · शायद। · हाँ, बाहर है। ·
  इनमें से कुछ नहीं। · नॉर्मल है। · घर से दूर। · घर की तरफ़। · हाँ, सही है।
- **Longer:** मेरा जनरेटर गैराज में चल रहा है, दरवाज़ा खुला है। · मैंने उसे बाहर रख दिया, घर से दूर। · पेट्रोल का एक
  टैंक कितनी देर चलता है? · दरवाज़ा साढ़े छह फीट ऊँचा है। · मुझे चक्कर आ रहे हैं।
- **English, a few** (switch the page to English first): Yes. · No. · I don't know. · Yes, I have one.

Voice turns don't need a row each in the manifest (the log keeps them in order): note who spoke, and any line skipped
or said again.

The coach's Hindi is as you approved it on Oct 29 (review page ec5466828411). «मुझे चक्कर आ रहे हैं।» gets the
emergency line, which now says «कार्बन मोनोऑक्साइड पॉइज़निंग»: listen to how the voice says it, and note any word it
says oddly, in that line or any other.

## 6. The check-in and the summary (a screen recording)

- After a conversation, in the **आज रात** card: set a time and tap **कैलेंडर में जोड़ें**; let the calendar sheet show,
  then cancel it (or add it and delete it afterwards).
- Tap **अभी जाँच करें** and answer: «नहीं, किसी को नहीं» · «हाँ, अभी भी बाहर है» · «हाँ, काम कर रहा है».
- Tap **परिवार को सारांश भेजें** and send it to your own number in Messages; record the message arriving on another
  phone if one is handy. Leave the note empty, or type something with no address in it.
